ROOT CAUSE (traced and confirmed — do not re-derive)
=============================================================================
The Move heading line reads av->getRenderRotation() = the OBJECT/drawable rotation (llviewerobject.cpp
:4599-4622), NOT the skeleton (mRoot). For the SELF avatar only, LLViewerObject::idleUpdate integrates
angular velocity every frame (applyAngularVelocity, llviewerobject.cpp:2538; self-only path gated at
llvoavatar.cpp:2880-2896). LLVOAvatar::updateOrientation latches mTurning and then sets
AGENT_CONTROL_TURN_RIGHT/LEFT EVERY FRAME while mTurning (llvoavatar.cpp:~4755-4813). mTurning normally
clears when the body finishes turning — but when a silently-active Actor Mover Move PINS mRoot each
frame (LLActorMover::applyOverride -> root->setWorldRotation, llactormover.cpp:1999-2000, called from
llvoavatar.cpp:5044 AFTER updateOrientation), the angle never closes, so mTurning stays latched forever,
the sim keeps turning the agent, angular velocity stays nonzero, and the OBJECT rotation spins
continuously (the line drifts on a timer / wraps 360) while the visible body stays put. The pinning Move
is created without pressing Walk: a Reset-to-Marks "hold" (placeAt, zero-speed, invisible, never
expires) or an ACTION-auto-started Loop walk (DirectorArmMoves default true).
