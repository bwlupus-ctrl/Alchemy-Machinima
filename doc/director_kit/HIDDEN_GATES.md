# Hidden gates: settings that silently suppress Director, Lightbox and Light Rig effects

Read-only audit of `indra/newview` (render, rig, weather, filters, Tron, Roto, atmosphere, diopter, VCam) against the Director kit XUI and `page_sources.json`. Sorted by how likely a user is to hit the gate. Full records (condition, file:line, settings, pages, status and action strings) are in `hidden_gates.json` next to this file.

Status column: **Shown** = the state is visible near the controls, **Partial** = only a greyed control or a page-level switch or pip, **No** = nothing tells the user.

## Notes

- **Dead or misleading controls (fix before adding banners):** row 7 (Prim glow does nothing in HDR), row 8 (`RenderBloomHDR` is never read), row 13 (switch absent from the Director card), row 15 (UNAVAILABLE text gives the wrong cause), row 10 (static "Requires Froxel Air" text).
- **Presets that do nothing without their master:** row 2 (Rim presets), row 12 (Roto presets write while Enable is off), row 18 (diopter presets).
- **Mockup pattern:** use the master banner from `mockup_src/Director.dc.html:355-361` ("X is off ... [Turn on]") above the affected cards. The v2 kit already has the page header switch and the rail and tab pip (`alkitshell.cpp:317-342`, `alkitpage.cpp:460-473`), so the missing piece is the local banner and the cross-page notes (rows 9 and 16).
- **Not verified:** whether Director v2 kit cards greyed or showed state for rows 4, 12, 14, 15, 18, 19; exact subject_source 0 result for row 20; whether the Shadows page greys projector rows.
- **Scope:** Light rig actor and avatar light response (AvatarLightResponseEnabled, polish) were checked only for kit masters and are not listed.
