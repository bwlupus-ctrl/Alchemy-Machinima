[postEffectUtilsF.glsl:790](I:/alchemy-machinima/indra/newview/app_settings/shaders/class1/alchemy/postEffectUtilsF.glsl:790), `:809` can turn that energy change into an immediate flare blink. **Duration: however many published dark frames occur.** Holding through missing frames cannot remove an authored brightness discontinuity.

Recommended design: separate **source lifetime/admission** from **playback-cycle state**. Preserve the accepted slot, cookie, load token, dimensions and volumetric owner during an admitted same-input loop; atomically replace pixels only after the next completed upload. Use seek-to-zero where supported, or repeat/restart semantics on the same validated input that also support unknown-duration files.

A **new file or actual player rebuild must still re-verify and remain muted/unpublished until admitted**; retain identity/token checks, pinned-file protections, policy gates and user-declined volumetric flags. If rebuilding is unavoidable, hold only previously admitted same-source pixels/ownership during the bounded verification interval—never publish replacement pixels or enable its audio early. Explicit Stop/failure/disable/replacement still clears through the existing teardown.
