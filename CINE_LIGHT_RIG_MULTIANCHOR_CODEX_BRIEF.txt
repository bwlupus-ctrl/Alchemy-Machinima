=============================================================================
WHAT IT IS (from the design)
=============================================================================
- The group is a subset of the 5 subject SLOTS {You, Subject A, B, C, D}: session state `mGroupEnabled`
  + a 5-bit `mGroupSlots` mask, beside the existing single `mAnchor`. Slots not UUIDs (recasting a
  subject re-lights the group; cap = 5 by construction).
- Group CENTRE = axis-aligned BOUNDS MIDPOINT of the resolved members' per-member points. Each member's
  point is its track-mode point (chest for TrackMode 0, render-pos+1.2 for TrackMode 1) through the
  UNCHANGED scaledPoint with THAT member's own getUniformScale(). Track-mode stays GLOBAL, composes per
  member. Lost members drop from the aggregate; zero resolved = today's dark path verbatim.
- Group SCALE = s_grp = max_i(s_i) + R_spread / r_nom, fed into the UNCHANGED Globals::mSubjectScale
  path (so effective_radius = clamp(r_nom*s_max + R_spread, floor, ceil); spread adds metres to the
  orbit; the existing 20 m cap applies for free). SA-9 preserved: mSubjectScale enters ONLY geometry,
  never the intensity/EV chain (verified: distance_ev reads NOMINAL safe_radius at
  alcinelightrigmodel.cpp:630; total_ev uses distance_ev not effective_radius at :656).
- Group FACING (for Mirror) = the PRIMARY resolved member (fixed slot order You->A->B->C->D). Single
  member = today's per-avatar facing exactly.
