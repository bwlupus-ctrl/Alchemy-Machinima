THE TRAPS — from the design + the Opus review corrections. Get these right; the review WILL probe them.
=============================================================================
1. BASIS REMAP (highest). Head/eye/neck LOCAL basis is PROVEN X=forward, Y=left, Z=up
   (llheadrotmotion.cpp:224, 245-247, 413-416; row-vector ctor llquaternion.cpp:95-103). Full-follow:
   fwd=(1,0,0)*R_joint; up=(0,0,1)*R_joint; right=fwd%up; up=right%fwd; mVirtualRot=
   LLQuaternion(right,up,-fwd) so (0,0,-1)*mVirtualRot==fwd. Match the existing "Snap to my view"
   construction (llfloaterprismmanager.cpp:1236).
2. CUSTOM JOINT (S1). The X=fwd/Z=up proof holds ONLY for spine-chain joints (head/eye/neck). A CUSTOM
   joint has no such guarantee (its local +X runs down the bone). So: a custom joint DEFAULTS TO
   STABILIZED (position-only) aim; full-follow on a custom joint is opt-in and documented best-effort.
   Head/eye/neck get full-follow normally.
3. LAZY JOINT ACCESSOR (S2). Use LLJoint::getWorldPosition()/getWorldRotation() (lazy-recompute,
   lljoint.cpp:743/813). NEVER getLastWorldPosition/getLastWorldRotation (:752/823, cached/stale) — the
   cached variant reads a one-frame-old pose despite correct tick ordering.
4. FOV SETTER (S3). Drive mFixedVerticalFovRad; the per-frame capture prep republishes it into
   mEffectiveVerticalFovRad (llprismlens.cpp:2734, 2788) which the render reads (:5601). Do NOT write
   mEffectiveVerticalFovRad directly, and do NOT gate the recompute behind a config-revision bump.
   Force EFovMode::FIXED (already mandatory for virtual cams, llprismlens.cpp:4096).
5. HORIZON-LOCK (D + N1). right=fwd%worldUp; up=right%fwd; guard |fwd·worldUp|>1-1e-3. On degeneracy,
   the PRIMARY fallback is HOLD LAST GOOD UP (not up0), so a straight-up look with a rolled head can't
   collapse the basis. Default roll-mode = horizon-lock. Inherit-roll keeps up=(0,0,1)*R_joint.
6. CLONE SCALE. position = scaledPoint(joint_world_pos, subject_scale) + (localOffset*scale)*R_joint,
   reusing the finalized foot-pivot scaledPoint() (alcinelightrig.cpp:85-109) and getUniformScale()
   (LLGhostAvatar override -> mEntityScale). Clones expose mHead/mEyeLeft/mEyeRight/mNeck (inherit
   LLVOAvatar). Eye joint = average of mEyeLeft+mEyeRight. Do NOT re-derive the scale math.
7. STABILIZED + TOGGLE HANDOFF. Stabilized drives ONLY mVirtualPos; mVirtualRot stays operator-owned
   (no per-frame contention). Seed the orientation slerp on an aim-mode/anchor/joint toggle so there is
   NO snap. Snap (mHave=false) on first frame / anchor change / joint change / mode change.
8. ANCHOR LOSS. Unresolved anchor/joint => HOLD LAST transform; never emit stale/NaN; re-acquire snaps
   fresh (no lerp from stale). No divide-by-zero.
9. SETTER SAFETY. The only per-frame render consumers of mVirtualPos/mVirtualRot are
   llprismlens.cpp:5569-5570 (aux) and :5227-5228 (guide); a new setVirtualCameraTransform() write-side
   helper is safe and leaves consumption byte-identical.
